# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 10, 2026

## Overview

For Your Service ("we", "our", "us") respects veteran privacy.

## Data Collection

### Personal Information
- Name, email, phone
- Military service history (MOS, branch, years)
- Skills and experience
- Location preferences
- Salary expectations

### Usage Data
- Job searches
- Matches viewed
- Application submissions
- Platform interactions

## Data Usage

We use data to:
- Match veterans with jobs
- Improve matching algorithms
- Analyze job market trends
- Communicate updates

## Data Sharing

We DO NOT sell veteran data.

We share with:
- Partner placement organizations (with consent)
- Prospective employers (when applying)
- Service providers (hosting, analytics)

## Data Security

- Encrypted at rest and in transit
- Access controls via Databricks
- Regular security audits
- GDPR/CCPA compliant

## Veteran Rights

You can:
- Access your data
- Correct inaccuracies
- Delete your account
- Opt-out of communications
- Export your data
